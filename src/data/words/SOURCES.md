# Word list sources and licences

Both lists are generated by `scripts/build-words.mjs` (`npm run words:build`) and committed as
`answers.ts` and `allowed.ts`. The generated files — not the npm packages — are the source of truth
at runtime, so the game needs no network access.

## Possible answers — `answers.ts` (1,922 words)

- **Source:** [SCOWL](http://wordlist.aspell.net/) by Kevin Atkinson, via the npm package
  [`wordlist-english`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/wordlist-english) 1.2.1 (MIT).
  SCOWL's own licence is a permissive MIT-style notice ("Permission to use, copy, modify,
  distribute and sell these word lists … is hereby granted without fee, provided that the above
  copyright notice appears in all copies"). Its frequency data derives in part from the public-domain
  Moby Words II lexicon.
- **Selection:** five-letter, all-lowercase words from SCOWL frequency tiers 10, 20 and 35
  (common words) in the general, American and British lists, then:
  - plurals, `-es` and past-tense (`-ed`) inflections of other known words are removed;
  - a hand-reviewed exclusion list removes offensive, sexual, bodily, slang-contraction, awkward
    comparative, `-ing`, proper-noun and regionally spelled words (e.g. `color`/`colour`), so no
    answer depends on the player's locale. These words may still be **accepted as guesses**.
- **Order:** shuffled once with a fixed seed (mulberry32, seed `20261001`). The order is the daily
  schedule — see "Changing the lists" below.

## Accepted guesses — `allowed.ts` (12,675 words)

- **Sources:** every five-letter word in [`word-list`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/word-list) 4.1.0
  by Sindre Sorhus (MIT), plus SCOWL tiers up to 50, plus all answers.
- This is a broad Scrabble-style list. It contains some obscure and some offensive words; accepting a
  guess does not mean the word can ever be an answer.

## Limitations

- This is not a complete English dictionary. Some valid but rare words, recent coinages and proper
  nouns are rejected as "Not in word list".
- Answer curation was done by filtering plus one manual review pass; a few answers may still feel
  obscure to some players.

## Changing the lists

Regenerating `answers.ts` changes the order of **all** future daily answers. To add answers after
launch, append them to the end of `answers.ts` by hand. If you deliberately replace the list, bump
`GAME.dictionaryVersion` in `src/config/game.ts`; saved daily sessions whose answer no longer matches
are discarded automatically.
