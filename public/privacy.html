Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026 · Applies to the Wordskiis website and the Wordskiis apps.

In short: the solo game stores everything on your own device. If you sign in with Google, a private copy of your progress is kept so it follows you between devices. If you use Friends, we store a display name you choose, a friend code, and your game results as coloured squares — never your guesses. We don’t show ads, sell data, or use tracking cookies.

Playing on your own

Daily Challenge, Practice, statistics and settings are saved in your browser or app on your device (local storage). Unless you sign in with Google (see Cloud save below), they are not sent to us. Clearing your browser or app data deletes them.

After a game ends, or when you open a word's meaning, that word (and nothing else) is sent to the free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) and to Wiktionary (wiktionary.org) to show its meaning.

Friends (optional)

If you open Friends, Wordskiis uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store:

  • An anonymous account identifier created for your device (no email or password needed).
  • The display name you choose and your 6-character friend code.
  • Your friends list (the players you added, or who added you).
  • Today’s Daily result and streak, and your challenge and race results, stored as colour patterns (for example green / yellow / grey) — not the letters you guessed.
  • The words used in challenges and races you play with friends.
  • In Word Swap: the words you pick, your guesses and what you’re typing while it’s your turn (so your friend can watch), the score, and nudges. Only the two players can see a match; either can end it, which deletes it.

Notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications, Wordskiis shows them on your device when a friend nudges you or it’s your turn in Word Swap. They are created on your device; no push messaging service is used.

Cloud save (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Wordskiis stores a copy of your game progress in Cloud Firestore so it follows you between devices: level and XP, achievements, personal bests, daily quests, Daily statistics and streak, today’s Daily Challenge board, your Word Book, your coins, shop purchases, streak freezes and daily chest, games in progress (such as your Tower run and Practice game) and your settings. Only you can read it. It is deleted with “Delete my profile”.

To protect Friends from abuse, Wordskiis uses Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise. When Friends connects, reCAPTCHA checks in the background that the request comes from the real Wordskiis website or app; Google receives technical information about your device and browser for this check, under the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. There is no puzzle to solve.

Your display name, friend code, Daily colour pattern and streak, and your profile (picture, frame, badge, title and banner) are visible to people who have your friend code or are on your friends list. Challenges and races are visible only to the two players involved. A gift you send (who it's from and to, and the coins or item) is visible only to you and the friend you send it to. If you choose a photo as your picture, Wordskiis shrinks it on your device to a small square (about 10 KB) and stores only that copy with your profile; remove it any time on your Profile page, and it is deleted with “Delete my profile”.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you choose “Save with Google” or “Sign in with Google”, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account’s email address, name and a unique identifier, so your profile can be restored on other devices. We use these only to sign you in. Your email is not shown to other players.

Deleting your data

In Friends → Delete my profile you can permanently delete your profile, friend code, friendships and sign-in account at any time. Past challenge and race records then contain only an anonymous identifier, with no name. Solo data can be removed by clearing the site or app data on your device. Step-by-step instructions, including how to delete your account without the app, are on Delete your account.

Where data is stored

Friends data is stored by Google Cloud (Firebase) in South Africa (Johannesburg). The website is hosted by Vercel. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Children

Wordskiis is a general-audience word game. It doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the optional display name described above, and it has no chat or messaging between players.

Contact
