Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026 · Applies to the Wordskiis website and the Wordskiis apps.

In short: the solo game stores everything on your own device. If you use Friends, we store a display name you choose, a friend code, and your game results as coloured squares — never your guesses. We don’t show ads, sell data, or use tracking cookies.

Playing on your own

Daily Challenge, Practice, statistics and settings are saved only in your browser or app on your device (local storage). They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser or app data deletes them.

After a game ends, the answer word (and nothing else) is sent to the free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) to show its meaning.

Friends (optional)

If you open Friends, Wordskiis uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store:

  • An anonymous account identifier created for your device (no email or password needed).
  • The display name you choose and your 6-character friend code.
  • Your friends list (the players you added, or who added you).
  • Today’s Daily result and streak, and your challenge and race results, stored as colour patterns (for example green / yellow / grey) — not the letters you guessed.
  • The words used in challenges and races you play with friends.

Your display name, friend code, Daily colour pattern and streak are visible to people who have your friend code or are on your friends list. Challenges and races are visible only to the two players involved.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you choose “Save with Google” or “Sign in with Google”, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account’s email address, name and a unique identifier, so your profile can be restored on other devices. We use these only to sign you in. Your email is not shown to other players.

Deleting your data

In Friends → Delete my profile you can permanently delete your profile, friend code, friendships and sign-in account at any time. Past challenge and race records then contain only an anonymous identifier, with no name. Solo data can be removed by clearing the site or app data on your device.

Where data is stored

Friends data is stored by Google Cloud (Firebase) in South Africa (Johannesburg). The website is hosted by Vercel. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Children

Wordskiis is a general-audience word game. It doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the optional display name described above, and it has no chat or messaging between players.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/leolennards/Wordskiis/issues.
