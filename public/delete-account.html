Delete your Wordskiis account

For the Wordskiis app on Google Play and the Wordskiis website.

You only have a Wordskiis account if you opened Friends or signed in with Google. Playing on your own needs no account, and that progress stays on your device.

In the app

  1. Open Wordskiis and tap Friends on the Home screen.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my profile.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

  1. Go to wordskiis.vercel.app/#friends in any browser.
  2. Tap Sign in with Google and use the same Google account as in the app.
  3. Tap Delete my profile and confirm.

If you never signed in with Google, your account only exists on the device you used. Delete it in the app as above, or uninstall the app: the account then can't be used again, and you can ask us to remove it by opening an issue at github.com/leolennards/Wordskiis/issues with your 6-character friend code (don't post anything else about yourself).

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, display name, friend code and profile (picture and title).
  • Your friends list and your cloud save (level, coins, items and game progress).
  • Your Daily result and streak as seen by friends.

Everything is deleted immediately; nothing is kept. Past challenge and race records that a friend still has then show only an anonymous identifier, with no name. Progress saved on your own device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

See also the privacy policy.
